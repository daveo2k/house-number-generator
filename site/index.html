Seal

Three Ø3 × 2 mm pins on the back of the face plate fit sockets in the diffuser. They're in three corners, so the plate only goes on one way round. Three snap catches inside the top of the shell click into notches in the back panel, and the grub screw locks the bottom. For the TPU option, use a plain length of 1.75 mm TPU filament, unprinted. The groove is sized for it, and the parts list gives the length to cut. Both ends go through two holes in the bottom of the rim and fold back into a pinch slot inside, so you don't need glue.

Fitting order

  1. Glue the face plate to the diffuser. The pins line it up.
  2. Screw the back panel to the wall and feed the cable through the gland.
  3. Connect the LED strip, which is stuck to the back panel.
  4. Fit the seal into the rim groove. For TPU filament, cut it to the length in the parts list. Push one end through an anchor hole at the bottom, fold it back and press it into the slot above. Stretch the filament round the groove, then anchor the other end in the second hole the same way.
  5. Push the shell over the rim until the three top catches click and the shell meets the wall.
  6. Tighten the grub screw from below with a 1.5 mm hex key.

Outdoors

Print in ASA or PETG for UV and heat resistance. PLA will warp on a sunny wall. A 2 mm weep hole in the bottom of the shell, near the front left corner, lets any condensation out. It sits in front of the back panel's rim, so the rim can't block it.

Letter islands

The centres of letters such as A, R, O and D are held by 1 mm bridges on the back of the face plate. They sit 2 mm behind the front face, so they're hard to see once the plaque is lit.
