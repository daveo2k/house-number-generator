Fitting order

  1. Screw the back panel to the wall and feed the cable through the gland.
  2. Connect the LED strip, which is stuck to the back panel.
  3. Push the shell over the rim and silicone cord until it meets the wall.
  4. Tighten the grub screw from below with a 1.5 mm hex key.

Outdoors

Print in ASA or PETG for UV and heat resistance. PLA will warp on a sunny wall. Leave a 2 mm weep hole at the lowest corner of the shell.

Letter islands

The centres of letters such as A, R, O and D are held by 1 mm bridges on the back of the face plate. The diffuser's raised letters are slotted around them, so the bridges can't be seen from the front.
